Deprecate `SolverVBD(particle_conservative_bound_relaxation=...)` and `rigid_conservative_bound_relaxation` in favor of the common `dat_conservative_bound_relaxation`, which scales soft-self and rigid-soft DAT truncation and their per-detection motion budgets. Either old name overrides the common value; if both old names are supplied, their values must agree. The value must lie in `(0, 1)`.
